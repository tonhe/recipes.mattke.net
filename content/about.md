I'm Tony Mattke. My day job is networks... I've been designing, deploying and fixing them since 1999, and I write about it over at RouterJockey. When I'm not doing that I'm usually cooking, and smoking a fair amount of meat (tri-tip, pork belly, brisket).

I've always wanted to put together a family cookbook. I'm a tech guy, so naturally the website came first. The book is still the goal.

The recipes here are mine, my Grandma Vi's, and the rest of the family's. Hers are copied exactly the way she wrote them, abbreviations and all, next to photos of the original cards. Anything that needed explaining is marked as a note, so you can always tell her words from mine.

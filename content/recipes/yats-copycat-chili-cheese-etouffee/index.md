---
title: "Yats Copycat Chili Cheese Étouffée"
summary: "A golden roux with the trinity and Rotel, simmered in stock, then made rich with cheddar, half and half and chili spices, with crawfish, shrimp or chicken over rice."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["etouffee", "cheese", "crawfish", "shrimp", "chicken", "copycat"]

ingredient_groups:
  - name: "Roux and vegetables"
    ingredients:
      - line: "1 stick butter"
      - line: "½ cup flour"
      - line: "1 cup chopped green onions"
      - line: "1 cup diced celery"
      - line: "1 cup diced bell peppers"
        note: "green are best, sweet peppers work"
      - line: "1 cup diced onion"
        note: "yellow"
      - line: "2 teaspoons minced garlic"
        note: "fresh is best; jarred works"
      - line: "1 cup Rotel Original diced tomatoes"
        alt: "or store-brand diced tomatoes and peppers"
  - name: "Seasoning and stock"
    ingredients:
      - line: "1 tablespoon tomato paste"
      - line: "½ teaspoon dried thyme"
      - line: "1 teaspoon dried basil"
      - line: "¼ teaspoon each black pepper, white pepper and cayenne"
      - line: "3 cups chicken stock or bouillon"
  - name: "Finish"
    ingredients:
      - line: "3 cups shredded cheddar"
      - line: "8 oz half and half or heavy cream"
      - line: "1 teaspoon chili powder"
      - line: "A pinch each of ground coriander, cumin and cloves"
      - line: "A dash of Worcestershire sauce"
      - line: "A dash of hot sauce"
        note: "Tabasco, or we use Sriracha"
      - line: "1 lb cooked crawfish, shrimp and/or chicken"
      - line: "White rice, for serving"

steps:
  - "**Roux:** melt the butter in a heavy skillet over medium heat and whisk in the flour. Keep stirring and scraping the bottom until it's golden; the more golden, the better."
  - "**Vegetables:** add the green onions, celery, bell peppers, onion, garlic and tomatoes. Cook until the onions brown."
  - "Stir in the tomato paste, thyme, basil and the three peppers."
  - "**Simmer:** add the stock, bring to a boil, then simmer until thick. If you double the recipe, use a little less stock or plan on a long simmer."
  - "**Finish:** stir in the cheese, half and half, chili powder, coriander, cumin, cloves, Worcestershire and hot sauce until smooth."
  - "Fold in the crawfish, shrimp or chicken and serve over white rice."

---
title: "Spring Fling Bacon"
summary: "Homemade bacon: pork belly dry-cured with maple sugar and pepper for a week, then smoked low at 150° and sliced thick."
people: ["Ryan Lee"]
categories: ["Snacks & sauces"]
tags: ["bacon", "pork", "smoking", "curing"]
yield: "About 7 lbs bacon from a 10 lb pork belly"
time: "5 to 6 hours at 150°"
time_label: "Smoke"

ingredient_groups:
  - ingredients:
      - line: "Pork belly"
      - line: "2-gallon ziplock bags"
  - name: "Cure, per 5 lbs of pork belly"
    ingredients:
      - line: "5 tablespoons Morton Curing Salt"
        note: "Always follow the instructions on the box"
      - line: "¼ cup sea salt"
      - line: "½ cup granulated maple syrup sugar"
      - line: "2 tablespoons pepper, or more based on taste"

steps:
  - "Trim pork belly."
  - "Cut into 5 lb pieces."
  - "Place in ziplock bags."
  - "Cover with curing mixture."
  - "Place in fridge."
  - "Flip daily for 7 days."
  - "Remove from bag and rinse twice."
  - "Add more pepper if desired."
  - "Lay on wire racks in fridge until pellicle forms, after 1 day."
  - "Smoke at 150° for 5 to 6 hours. If you go too high, it will start to render the fat and you will be left with lean bacon."
  - "Cool on the counter for 2 hours, then place in the fridge for 24 hours to let it solidify."
  - "Slice thick."

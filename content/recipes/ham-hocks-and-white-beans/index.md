title: "Slow Cooker Smoked Ham Hocks & Creamy White Beans"
summary: "My pork and beans: smoked ham hocks and navy beans slow-cooked all day, finished with jarred Great Northerns and mashed beans for body."

categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["beans", "ham", "slow cooker", "soup"]
time: "8-10 hours on low"

ingredients:
  - line: "2.5-3 lb smoked ham hocks"
  - line: "1 lb dried navy beans"
  - line: "1 jar (48 oz) Randall Great Northern beans, undrained"
  - line: "4 cups low-sodium chicken stock"
  - line: "2-4 cups water, as needed"
  - line: "2 medium yellow onions, diced"
  - line: "2 tbsp minced garlic"
    note: "jarlic"
  - line: "2 bay leaves"
  - line: "1 tsp dried thyme"
  - line: "1 tsp coarse black pepper"
  - line: "¼ tsp smoked paprika"
    note: "optional"
  - line: "Salt"
    note: "to taste at the end"
  - line: "Mezzetta pepper vinegar"
    note: "to finish individual bowls"

steps:
  - "**The night before: Soak the navy beans.** Sort and rinse the dried navy beans. Cover with at least 3 inches of cold water and refrigerate for 8-12 hours. Drain and rinse in the morning."
  - "**Morning: Load the crockpot.** Add the soaked navy beans, smoked ham hocks, onions, garlic, chicken stock, 2 cups water, bay leaves, thyme, and black pepper. Add enough additional water to keep the beans submerged by about an inch, without overfilling the crockpot. Leave the Randall beans out for now."
  - "**Slow cook all day.** Cook on LOW for approximately 8-10 hours, or longer if necessary, until the navy beans are fully tender and the ham hocks are falling apart. Avoid repeatedly lifting the lid. If the beans are still firm, keep cooking. Slow cookers and dried beans vary considerably."
  - "**About an hour before serving: Pull the ham.** Remove the hocks and let them cool enough to handle. Strip off the usable meat, shred or chop it, and return it to the crockpot. Discard the bones, tough skin, and excess fat. If you like some of the soft gelatinous skin, finely chop a little and return it for extra body."
  - "**Add the Randall beans and make it creamy.** Add the entire 48-ounce jar of Randall Great Northern beans, including the liquid. Mash 1-2 cups of the cooked navy beans against the side of the crockpot, then stir everything together. Cook on HIGH for another 30-60 minutes, until the added beans are hot and the broth has thickened. Make sure the soup is piping hot throughout."
  - "**Taste and serve.** Remove the bay leaves. Taste before adding any salt because the smoked hocks may have seasoned the entire pot. Add a little more black pepper if needed. Serve with cornbread."

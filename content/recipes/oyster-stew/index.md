---
title: "Oyster Stew"
summary: "Oysters in milk with celery and onion sautéed in butter, seasoned with Worcestershire, salt and pepper."
people: ["Grandma Vi"]
categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["oysters", "soup", "flip book"]
time_label: "Cook"

ingredients:
  - line: "1/3 C finely chopped celery"
  - line: "2-3 T finely chopped onion"
  - line: "Butter or oleo (1/2 stick)"
  - line: "1 1/2 qts milk"
  - line: "oysters"
    editor: "Perry says 8 oz cans, probably eight of them."
  - line: "1 tsp Worcestershire sauce"
  - line: "Salt & pepper to taste"

steps:
  - "Saute (onions) & (celery) in Butter or oleo (1/2 stick) (don't let Brown – About 5 min total)"
  - "Combine 1 1/2 qts milk & onions"
  - "Bring Almost to a Boil (slowly)"
  - "Add oysters. 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce, Salt & pepper to taste"
  - "Cook Almost to A Boil"

  - line: "1c fine sugar"

  - "mix 1c milk 3T flour"
  - "Boil til smooth · Cool – set aside"
  - "To 1c butter – 2 sticks (soft. add slowly 1c fine sugar"
  - "beat continuously"
  - "slowly add flour mixture"
  - "beat until fluffy"

---
title: "The Best Green Bean Casserole"
summary: "Fresh green beans and browned mushrooms in a garlic and parmesan béchamel, baked and topped with French's fried onions."
people: ["Tony"]
categories: ["Sides"]
tags: ["green beans", "mushrooms", "casserole"]
oven: "375°F"
time: "25-30"

ingredients:
  - line: "2 lbs fresh green beans"
  - line: "Kosher Salt"
  - line: "2 Tbsp EVOO"
  - line: "1 lb. crimini / mini bella mushrooms – sliced"
  - line: "6 Tbsp Unsalted Butter"
  - line: "4 large Thyme sprigs"
  - line: "2 Tbsp AP Flour"
  - line: "1¼ C Whole Milk"
  - line: "1 C Heavy Cream"
  - line: "4 garlic cloves – fine chop"
  - line: "½ C Grated parmesan cheese"
  - line: "Coarse / fresh Black Pepper"
  - line: "3/4 C French's Onions (fried)"

steps:
  - "Par boil beans in salted water in batches until bright green and half tender ~ 3min"
  - "Cook Mushrooms in EVOO (half at time) No stir 2min – until golden – then 3min"
  - "Add 2Tbsp Butter, 2 sprig thyme (per batch) Cook 4min until dark and very tender"
  - "Melt 2Tbsp Butter in Saucepan – med low"
  - "Whisk in 2Tbsp AP Flour – cook until roux is golden brown ~ 4mins"
  - "Whisk in Whole Milk and Heavy Cream"
  - "Increase heat – Whisk often while simmer until thick and bubbly ~ 5 mins"
  - "Remove from heat, add garlic, parm. cheese and season w/ Salt + Pepper."
  - "Arrange Beans and Mushrooms in baking dish – pour over bechamel"
  - "Cover w/ foil – Bake @ 375°F for 25-30 (until sauce bubbling)"
  - "Cover w/ fried Onions – Bake 3 mins until they darken slightly"

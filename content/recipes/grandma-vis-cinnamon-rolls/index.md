steps:

  - "Cover and let rise overnight in refrigerator or until double in size."
  - "Roll out on a lightly floured board 1/2-3/4\" thick. Brush with melted butter. Sprinkle with brown sugar and cinnamon. Roll up and cut 3/4\" slices. Arrange in buttered pan and cover with dishtowel. Let rise to double in size and bake in pre-heated oven 375 degrees for 15-20 minutes."

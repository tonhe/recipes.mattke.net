---
title: "Viola's Pork Chops"
summary: "Mayonnaise-brushed pork chops in a saltine and onion soup crust."
people: ["Grandma Vi"]
categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["pork"]
yield: "8 servings"
oven: "425°"
time: "35 to 40 min"

source:
  where: "The Spokesman-Review · February 26, 2014"
  label: "We don't have Grandma's card for this one. Aunt Teddy shared it with the paper in 2014, and the steps below are our retelling of her write-up."
  url: "https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2014/feb/26/in-the-kitchen-with-teddy-bernier-cottage-potatoes/"

ingredients:
  - line: "2 cups crushed saltines"
  - line: "2 packages dried onion soup mix"
  - line: "8 pork chops, fat trimmed off"
  - line: "2 to 3 tablespoons mayonnaise"

steps:
  - "Mix the crushed saltines and onion soup mix in a medium bowl or a zip-top bag."
  - "Spread a thin layer of mayonnaise on both sides of each chop."
  - "Coat each chop in the saltine mixture."
  - "Bake at 425° for 35 to 40 minutes, turning the chops after 20 minutes."

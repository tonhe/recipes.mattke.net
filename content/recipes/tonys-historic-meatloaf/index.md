---
title: "Tony's Historic Meatloaf"
summary: "A beef and pork meatloaf with panko, chili powder and thyme, baked on a sheet pan and brushed with a sweet catsup, honey and brown sugar topping."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["beef", "pork", "meatloaf"]
oven: "350°"
time: "50-60 Minutes"

ingredient_groups:
  - ingredients:
      - line: "3lbs Meat (2lbs 90/10 beef 1lb pork or Meatloaf Mix or all Ground beef)"
      - line: "1 cup Panko bread crumbs"
      - line: "1 egg"
      - line: "1/4 C Milk"
      - line: "½ onion minced (large sweet)"
      - line: "2 tsp chili powder"
      - line: "4 Cloves garlic - Minced"
      - line: "1½ tsp Kosher Salt"
      - line: "1 tblsp Mustard"
      - line: "1 tblsp Worcestershire"
      - line: "2 tsp black pepper"
      - line: "1 tsp dried thyme"
  - name: "Topping"
    ingredients:
      - line: "3/4 C Catsup"
      - line: "1 tsp ground cumin"
      - line: "dash Hot Sauce & Worcestershire"
      - line: "1 tblsp Honey"
      - line: "4 tblsp brown Sugar"

steps:
  - "PreHeat Oven @ 350° – Mix ingredients for loaf in a large bowl, add Meat Spices, eggs, milk followed by bread crumbs."
  - "Mix well, but avoid squeezing the meat."
  - "Shape into \"loaf\" place on parchment lined sheet pan."
  - "Bake for 50-60 Minutes or until internal temp reaches 155°."
  - "let rest 10 minutes – cover with topping."
  - "– Warm Sauce in saucepan and brush lightly over Meatloaf 20 minutes into cooking."

  - line: "1T lemon juice"
    note: "If you like lemon"
  - line: "prepared pie shell – either baked or graham cracker"

steps:
  - "Dissolve 1 pkg Knox gelatin in 3T water"
  - "Let stand 5 minutes"
  - "Add 1-3 oz pkg strawberry jello"
  - "Pour in 1 c boiling water"
  - "stir til dissolved"
  - "Add ½ c sugar to this"
  - "In blender place the jello slowly add 1 lb strawberries as the blender is going – then add 1-8 oz pkg cream cheese –"
  - "Let set until it starts to thicken"
  - "(If you like lemon you may add 1T lemon juice)"
  - "Pour in prepared pie shell – either baked or graham cracker – Chill at least 2 hrs –"
  - "Decorate with whipped"

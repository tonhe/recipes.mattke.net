---
title: "“Southern” Italian Pasta e Fagioli"
subtitle: "Slow Cooker Beef, Hot Italian Sausage, White Beans & Mustard Greens"
summary: "A thick, hearty pasta e fagioli with ground beef, hot Italian sausage, beans, tomatoes, and greens. Serve the pasta separately so leftovers stay soup-like, and finish each bowl with Mezzetta hot chili pepper brine."
people: ["Tony"]
categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["soup", "sausage", "beans", "pasta", "slow cooker"]
yield: "8–10 servings"
prep: "20–25 minutes"
time: "4–5 hours on HIGH or 6–7 hours on LOW"
time_label: "Slow cooker"

ingredient_groups:
  - name: "Meat"
    ingredients:
      - line: "½ lb lean ground beef, preferably 90/10"
      - line: "½ lb hot Italian sausage, removed from casings if needed"
      - line: "1 tbsp olive oil, only if needed"
  - name: "Vegetables"
    ingredients:
      - line: "1 medium yellow onion, diced"
      - line: "2 medium carrots, cut into ½- to ¾-inch pieces"
      - line: "2 celery stalks, cut into ½- to ¾-inch pieces"
      - line: "6 cloves garlic, minced"
      - line: "1 hearty bunch flat-leaf mustard greens (about 8–12 oz), thick stems removed and leaves chopped"
        note: "Curly-leaf mustard greens are not recommended. Collards can be used in a pinch."
  - name: "Beans and tomatoes"
    ingredients:
      - line: "2 cans (15 oz each) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed"
      - line: "1 can (15 oz) dark red kidney beans, drained and rinsed"
      - line: "1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes"
      - line: "1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, with juices"
      - line: "2 tbsp tomato paste"
  - name: "Broth, pasta, and seasonings"
    ingredients:
      - line: "2½ cups low-sodium beef broth, plus more as needed"
      - line: "1 cup dry ditalini pasta"
      - line: "2 tsp Italian seasoning"
      - line: "1 tsp dried oregano"
      - line: "½ tsp dried thyme"
      - line: "½ tsp smoked paprika"
      - line: "¼ tsp crushed red pepper flakes"
        note: "optional"
      - line: "2 bay leaves"
      - line: "½ tsp freshly ground black pepper"
      - line: "Salt, to taste"
  - name: "To serve"
    ingredients:
      - line: "Mezzetta hot chili pepper brine, to taste"
      - line: "Chopped Mezzetta hot chili peppers"
        note: "optional"
      - line: "Freshly grated Parmesan"
      - line: "Fresh Italian parsley"
      - line: "Crusty Italian bread"

steps:
  - "**Brown the meat.** Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef and hot Italian sausage. Cook, breaking the meat into small crumbles, until browned and cooked through. Let it sit undisturbed for short stretches so it develops some browning. Drain excess grease, then transfer the meat to the slow cooker."
  - "**Sauté the vegetables.** If the skillet is dry, add a little olive oil. Add the onion, carrots, and celery. Cook for 5–7 minutes, until the onion begins to soften. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for another 1–2 minutes. Transfer the vegetables to the slow cooker."
  - "**Assemble the slow cooker.** Add the crushed tomatoes, diced tomatoes and their juices, cannellini beans, kidney beans, beef broth, Italian seasoning, oregano, thyme, smoked paprika, red pepper flakes if using, black pepper, and bay leaves. For a thicker consistency, mash about ½ cup of the cannellini beans with a fork before adding them. Stir everything together. Cover and cook on HIGH for about 4 hours or LOW for 6–7 hours, until the vegetables are tender."
  - "**Add the mustard greens.** On HIGH, stir in the chopped flat-leaf mustard greens after about 2½ hours of cooking. Cover and continue cooking for about 90 minutes, until the greens are tender. On LOW, add the mustard greens during the last 1½–2 hours. If using collards, add them earlier because they usually need more time to soften."
  - "**Check the consistency and seasoning.** About 30 minutes before serving, stir the soup and check that the carrots, celery, and greens are tender. If it is thicker than you like, add beef broth ½ cup at a time. Taste before adding salt. The sausage, broth, Parmesan, and pepper brine can all add saltiness."
  - "**Cook the pasta separately.** Cook the ditalini in a pot of salted water according to the package directions, until al dente. Drain and set aside. Keeping the pasta separate helps prevent it from soaking up the soup broth in the refrigerator."
  - "**Finish and serve.** Remove the bay leaves. Ladle the soup into bowls over about ⅓–½ cup cooked ditalini. Top with Parmesan and parsley. Add Mezzetta hot chili pepper brine to each bowl to taste. Chopped pickled peppers are good too. Start with a little, then add more at the table."

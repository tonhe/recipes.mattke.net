---
title: "Shrimp and Grits"
summary: "Creamy stone-ground grits cooked in broth and milk with sharp cheddar, topped with shrimp seared in bacon fat, a butter pan sauce and crumbled bacon."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["shrimp", "grits", "bacon", "southern"]
time: "10 to 15 min"
time_label: "Simmer grits"

ingredient_groups:
  - name: "Grits"
    ingredients:
      - line: "2 cups chicken broth"
      - line: "2 cups whole milk"
      - line: "3 tablespoons butter"
      - line: "¾ cup stone-ground grits"
      - line: "Salt, to taste"
      - line: "1 cup shredded sharp cheddar"
  - name: "Shrimp"
    ingredients:
      - line: "4 strips bacon, or 2 to 3 thick-cut slices"
      - line: "1 pound large or jumbo shrimp, peeled and deveined"
      - line: "Salt and black pepper, to taste"
      - line: "1 tablespoon butter"
      - line: "½ cup chicken broth"

steps:
  - "**Grits:** bring 2 cups of broth, the milk, 3 tablespoons of butter and a pinch of salt to a gentle boil in a medium saucepan. Whisk in the grits."
  - "Turn the heat down and simmer 10 to 15 minutes, whisking every few minutes, until creamy and tender. Some grits take longer; go by the package if it says so. Cover, take off the heat, and let them sit."
  - "**Bacon:** meanwhile, fry the bacon in a skillet until crisp. Drain on paper towels, then crumble. Pour off all but about 2 tablespoons of the fat."
  - "**Shrimp:** pat the shrimp dry, season with salt and pepper, and cook in the bacon fat about 2 minutes a side, just until done."
  - "**Pan sauce:** add the last tablespoon of butter and ½ cup of broth to the skillet and stir until the butter melts and it's hot. Taste and adjust the seasoning."
  - "Stir the cheddar into the grits until melted. Add salt if it needs it."
  - "Spoon the grits into bowls, top with the shrimp and pan sauce, and sprinkle on the bacon. Serve right away."

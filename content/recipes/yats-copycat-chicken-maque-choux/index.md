---
title: "Yats Copycat Chicken Maque Choux"
summary: "Caramelized corn and shredded chicken in a brown roux with the trinity, tomato paste, chicken broth, cream and Cajun seasoning."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["chicken", "corn", "maque choux", "copycat"]

ingredient_groups:
  - name: "Chicken"
    ingredients:
      - line: "2 to 3 chicken breasts"
      - line: "Chicken stock, for poaching"
  - name: "Roux and vegetables"
    ingredients:
      - line: "8 tablespoons butter"
      - line: "½ cup flour"
      - line: "1 to 1½ cups onion, green pepper and celery, chopped and combined"
      - line: "1 tablespoon garlic"
      - line: "¼ cup tomato paste"
  - name: "Liquid and seasoning"
    ingredients:
      - line: "3 cups chicken broth"
      - line: "½ cup heavy cream"
      - line: "Garlic powder, onion powder, paprika and Cajun seasoning"
      - line: "Salt and pepper"
      - line: "2 tablespoons brown sugar"
  - name: "Corn"
    ingredients:
      - line: "1 cup corn"
      - line: "1 teaspoon to 1 tablespoon honey"
        note: "only if you don't caramelize the corn"

steps:
  - "**Chicken:** cook the breasts in chicken stock until done, then shred."
  - "**Corn:** caramelize the corn in a hot skillet with a little butter until it's browned in spots. Don't skip this; if you do, you'll need the honey at the end."
  - "**Roux:** melt the butter, whisk in the flour, and cook until brown."
  - "Add the onion, green pepper and celery, then the garlic and the tomato paste."
  - "Slowly stir in the chicken broth and the cream."
  - "Season with garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, Cajun seasoning, salt, pepper and the brown sugar."
  - "Stir in the corn and the shredded chicken and let it heat through."
  - "If the corn wasn't caramelized, finish with a swirl of honey."
---

---
title: "Italian Sausage, White Bean & Mustard Green Soup"
summary: "Hearty, savory, creamy without cream or pasta."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["soup", "sausage", "beans", "slow cooker"]
yield: "Approximately 8-10 servings"
time: "LOW 5-6 hours or HIGH 3-4 hours"
time_label: "Cook"

ingredients:
  - line: "1 lb hot or sweet Italian sausage, casings removed"
  - line: "3 cans (15 oz each) Great Northern or cannellini beans, drained and rinsed"
  - line: "1 large onion, diced"
  - line: "4 carrots, diced"
  - line: "4 celery stalks, diced"
  - line: "6-8 cloves garlic, minced"
  - line: "6 cups low-sodium chicken stock"
  - line: "1-2 tbsp olive oil, as needed"
  - line: "1 tbsp tomato paste"
    note: "optional"
  - line: "1 tsp dried rosemary, crushed"
    alt: "or 1 fresh sprig"
  - line: "1 tsp dried thyme"
  - line: "2 bay leaves"
  - line: "½ tsp smoked paprika"
    note: "optional"
  - line: "1 large bunch mustard greens, roughly chopped"
    note: "the hearty flat leaf kind, not the curly leaf"
  - line: "Black pepper and salt to taste"
  - line: "Hot pepper vinegar to finish (per bowl)"
  - line: "Parmesan for serving"

steps:
  - "Brown the sausage thoroughly in a skillet. Break it into fairly substantial chunks. Transfer it to the slow cooker."
  - "Sauté the onion, carrots, and celery in the remaining sausage fat for 5-7 minutes. Add garlic and tomato paste for the final minute."
  - "Transfer everything to the slow cooker with the drained beans, chicken stock, rosemary, thyme, bay leaves, and paprika."
  - "Cook on LOW for 5-6 hours or HIGH for 3-4 hours, until the vegetables are tender."
  - "Add chopped mustard greens during the final 45-60 minutes, depending on how tender you want them."
  - "Mash about 1 cup of beans into the broth. This gives you a thicker, creamier soup without adding flour."
  - "Finish each bowl with pepper vinegar, adjust seasoning, and serve."

editor_notes:
  - "Make it in the slow cooker, using the stovetop just to brown the sausage and sauté the vegetables."
---

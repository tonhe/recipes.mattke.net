title: "Superb Scalloped potatoes"
summary: "Thin-sliced russets layered with salt and half-and-half, then finished with heavy cream and baked until golden."
people: ["Grandma Vi"]
categories: ["Sides"]
tags: ["potatoes", "casserole", "cream"]
oven: "325°"
title_image: title.png

ingredients:
  - line: "2# russet potatoes sliced thin"
    editor: "# = pounds."
  - line: "1 ½ tsp salt"
  - line: "1½ c ½ – ½"
    editor: "½ – ½ = half-and-half."
  - line: "½ c heavy cream"

steps:
  - "Butter well a 2 qt casserole"
  - "Layer potatoes – salting each layer –"
  - "Pour half + half over – dot with 1T butter"
  - "Bake 45 mins"
  - "Spoon cream over continue baking 45 min more – or until golden brown"

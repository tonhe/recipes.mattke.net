title: "Potato Salad"
summary: "Sliced boiled potatoes with vinegar, mustard, Miracle Whip, onion, red bell pepper and radishes, mixed with chopped eggs and topped with chives and paprika."
people: ["Grandma Vi"]
categories: ["Sides"]
tags: ["potatoes", "salad", "eggs"]
yield: "Serves 6-8"
title_image: title.png

ingredients:
  - line: "8 c boiled potatoes (sliced)"
  - line: "½ c white vinegar"
  - line: "⅓ c sugar + 1T"
  - line: "2 tsp salt"
  - line: "2 T yellow mustard"
  - line: "2 c Miracle Whip"
    note: "no sub"
  - line: "½ c finely chopped onion"
  - line: "2-3 T chives"
    alt: "or onion tops"
  - line: "¼ c red bell pepper (ripe) cut ¼ in long"
  - line: "3-4 large radishes"
    editor: "Not on the card. Perry remembers her adding these."
  - line: "8-9 boiled chopped eggs"

steps:
  - "Mix well – let set a few minutes – then mix in 8-9 boiled chopped eggs."
  - "mix – sprinkle top with chives and paprika"
  - "For best flavor serve room temperature"

author_note: "Potatoes are best boiled with skins – peel when cool – they are less moist."

---
title: "Tony's Amazing Chocolate Chip Oatmeal Cookies"
summary: "Oatmeal cookies with semi-sweet and milk chocolate chips, baked on parchment until just brown at the edges."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Cookies & bars"]
tags: ["cookies", "oatmeal", "chocolate"]
oven: "375°"
time: "8-9 Minutes"

ingredients:
  - line: "2c all-purp. Flour"
  - line: "1 tsp baking powder"
  - line: "1 tsp baking Soda"
  - line: "½ tsp Salt"
  - line: "1 c butter, softened"
  - line: "3/4 c white sugar"
  - line: "3/4 c brown sugar - packed"
  - line: "1 egg + 1 egg yolk"
  - line: "2 tsp Vanilla extract"
  - line: "2c rolled Oats"
  - line: "½ c Semi-Sweet Choc. Chips"
    note: "Use Ghirardelli"
  - line: "3/4 c Milk Choc. chips"
    note: "Use Ghirardelli"

steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 375°"
  - "Sift flour, baking powder & soda, and salt – Set aside."
  - "In a large bowl cream butter and white / brown sugar until light and fluffy."
  - "Beat in eggs one at a time, then Stir in Vanilla."
  - "Gradually add sifted ingredients."
  - "Stir in Oats and chocolate."
  - "Drop rounded tablespoons onto parchment lined cookie Sheets."
  - "Bake 8-9 Minutes until slightly brown on edges."
  - "Allow to cool 5 mins before removing. (They will crumble!)"

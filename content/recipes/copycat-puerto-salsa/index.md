---
title: "Copycat Puerto Salsa"
summary: "Restaurant-style salsa: half blended smooth, half chopped and stirred in, with cilantro, jalapeño and lime, rested overnight."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
credit: "[Tony Mattke](/people/tony-mattke/)'s copy of Ryan Lee's recipe."
categories: ["Snacks & sauces"]
tags: ["salsa", "tomatoes", "cilantro"]
time: "overnight"
time_label: "Chill"

ingredients:
  - line: "4 Roma tomatoes"
  - line: "1 small onion"
  - line: "1 can diced tomatoes"
  - line: "1 bunch cilantro"
  - line: "1 jalapeño"
  - line: "1 tablespoon garlic powder"
  - line: "1 tablespoon finely chopped garlic"
  - line: "1 tablespoon onion powder"
  - line: "1 tablespoon chili powder"
  - line: "1 teaspoon cayenne pepper"
  - line: "1 teaspoon baking soda"
  - line: "2 tablespoons olive oil"
  - line: "Roughly 2 teaspoons salt, plus more to taste"
  - line: "Juice of one lime"

steps:
  - "Rough chop three Roma tomatoes and place in blender. Chop one Roma tomato and set aside to add later."
  - "Chop one small onion and add half to blender and set aside the other half for later."
  - "Add one can of diced tomatoes."
  - "Chop one bunch of cilantro. Add half and set the other half aside for later."
  - "Chop one jalapeño. Add half and set the other half aside for later."
  - "Blend until well blended."
  - "Add the rest of the tomato, onion, cilantro, and jalapeño."
  - "Add 1 tablespoon garlic powder, 1 tablespoon finely chopped garlic, 1 tablespoon onion powder, 1 tablespoon chili powder, 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper, 1 teaspoon baking soda, 2 tablespoons olive oil, and roughly 2 teaspoons of salt."
  - "Add juice of one lime after salsa sat for 30 minutes."
  - "Place in fridge overnight."
  - "Add more salt to taste."

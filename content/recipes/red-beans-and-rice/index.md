---
title: "New Orleans Red Beans and Rice"
summary: "Camellia red beans simmered for hours with a smoked ham hock, andouille and the trinity, finished with butter and Crystal, over buttered rice."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["beans", "rice", "andouille", "ham hock", "new orleans"]
time: "2 to 3 hours, plus an overnight soak"
time_label: "Simmer"

ingredient_groups:
  - name: "Beans"
    ingredients:
      - line: "1 lb dried red kidney beans"
        note: "Camellia brand – it has to be Camellia"
      - line: "1 tablespoon Morton table salt and 6 cups cold water, for soaking"
      - line: "1 smoked ham hock"
      - line: "12 oz andouille sausage, diced small"
        alt: "or kielbasa"
      - line: "1 tablespoon neutral oil"
      - line: "1 large yellow onion, diced"
      - line: "2 celery ribs, diced"
      - line: "1 green bell pepper, diced"
      - line: "4 garlic cloves, minced"
      - line: "½ teaspoon ground white pepper"
      - line: "¼ teaspoon ground black pepper"
      - line: "¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper"
      - line: "1 teaspoon dried thyme"
      - line: "3 bay leaves"
      - line: "8 cups water, plus hot water as needed"
  - name: "Finish"
    ingredients:
      - line: "4 tablespoons unsalted butter"
      - line: "Up to 2 teaspoons Morton table salt, to taste"
      - line: "1 tablespoon Crystal or Louisiana Hot Sauce"
        note: "a must"
  - name: "Rice"
    ingredients:
      - line: "2 cups popcorn rice"
        alt: "or basmati or jasmine rice"
      - line: "3 cups water"
      - line: "½ teaspoon Morton table salt"
      - line: "2 bay leaves"
      - line: "1 tablespoon unsalted butter"

steps:
  - "**Soak.** Pick out any stones or shriveled beans and rinse. Stir the salt into 6 cups of cold water, add the beans, and soak 8 to 16 hours. Drain and rinse."
  - "**Brown the andouille.** Heat the oil in a large heavy pot over medium heat and brown the sausage until a little crisp. Lift it out and leave the fat in the pot."
  - "**Caramelize half the vegetables.** Mix the onion, celery and bell pepper and split them in half. Cook one half over medium to medium-high until deeply browned, without burning."
  - "**Garlic and spices.** Add the garlic for 20 to 30 seconds, then the white pepper, black pepper and cayenne for 10 to 20 seconds more."
  - "**Deglaze.** Pour in ½ cup of the water and scrape up the browned bits. Add the rest of the vegetables, the thyme and the bay leaves."
  - "**Simmer.** Add the beans, ham hock and the remaining 7½ cups of water. Bring to a boil, then drop to a gentle simmer with the lid ajar, stirring now and then and adding hot water to keep the beans covered. Cook until soft all the way through, 2 to 3 hours."
  - "**Rice.** Bring the rice, water, salt, bay leaves and butter to a boil in a saucepan, cover, and cook on low 18 minutes without lifting the lid. Rest off the heat, covered, 10 minutes. Pull the bay leaves and fluff."
  - "**Thicken.** For the last 30 to 45 minutes, mash some of the beans against the side of the pot and stir them in. Take out the ham hock, shred the meat, and put it back without the skin and bone. Stir the andouille back in for the last 30 minutes."
  - "**Finish.** Stir in the butter for the last 5 minutes. Taste before salting, since the hock and sausage season the pot, then add salt a little at a time. Stir in the hot sauce and take out the bay leaves."
  - "**Serve.** Ladle the beans into bowls and spoon the rice on top."
---

Leftovers keep 3 to 4 days in the fridge. The beans thicken as they cool, so loosen them with a splash of water when you reheat.

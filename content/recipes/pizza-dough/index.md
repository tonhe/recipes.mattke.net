---
title: "Pizza Dough"
summary: "A small batch of pizza dough: flour, warm water and a packet of yeast, olive oil, and a dash of salt and plenty of black pepper."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Breads & rolls"]
tags: ["pizza", "dough", "bread"]
show_photos: false
yield: "One 12-14 inch pizza"
oven: "475°"
time: "12-15 min"

ingredients:
  - line: "2½ cups flour"
  - line: "¾ cup warm water"
    note: "105-115°"
  - line: "1 packet yeast"
  - line: "2 tbl olive oil"
  - line: "Salt & pepper"
    note: "a dash of each, and I like to add extra black pepper"

steps:
  - "Stir the yeast into the warm water and let it sit about 5 minutes, until foamy."
  - "Add the olive oil, salt and pepper."
  - "Mix in the flour a little at a time until a soft dough forms."
  - "Knead on a floured counter until smooth and elastic, about 8-10 minutes. Add a little flour if it's sticky."
  - "Put it in an oiled bowl, cover, and let it rise in a warm spot until doubled, 1 to 1½ hours."
  - "Punch it down and stretch or roll it out on a greased or floured pan."
  - "Top it and bake at 475° for 12-15 minutes, until the crust is golden."

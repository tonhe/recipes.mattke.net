---
title: "Blackberry Grunt"
summary: "Blackberries cooked down with sugar in a cast iron skillet, topped with buttermilk biscuit dumplings and baked until the tops start to brown."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Cakes & pies"]
tags: ["blackberries", "cobbler", "cast iron", "dessert"]
oven: "400°"
time: "15 to 20 min"

ingredient_groups:
  - name: "Dumplings"
    ingredients:
      - line: "2 cups flour"
      - line: "2 teaspoons baking powder"
      - line: "1 teaspoon kosher salt"
      - line: "¼ teaspoon baking soda"
      - line: "2 ounces unsalted butter, cut into small pieces and chilled"
      - line: "1 cup buttermilk"
  - name: "Filling"
    ingredients:
      - line: "4 cups blackberries"
      - line: "1 cup sugar"
      - line: "1 cup water"

steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 400°. Put a sheet of foil on the bottom rack to catch drips."
  - "**Dumplings:** pulse the flour, baking powder, salt and baking soda in a food processor 3 or 4 times, then tip it into a large bowl."
  - "Rub the butter in with your hands until about half of it disappears and the rest is in pea-size bits."
  - "Make a well, pour in the buttermilk, and stir with a spatula or wooden spoon just until it comes together."
  - "Turn it out onto lightly floured parchment or waxed paper, shape into a ball, wrap, and refrigerate while you make the filling."
  - "**Filling:** stir the blackberries, sugar and water together and pour into a 10-inch cast iron skillet over medium heat."
  - "Bring to a simmer, turn down to medium-low, and cook about 15 minutes, stirring now and then, until the juice coats the back of a spoon."
  - "Drop the dough over the fruit in spoonfuls (a 1-ounce scoop is about right), spread evenly across the top."
  - "Bake 15 to 20 minutes, until the tops just start to brown."
  - "Let it cool 15 to 30 minutes before serving."

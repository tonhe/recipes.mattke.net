steps:
  - "Separate 4 eggs and let set at room temp. – 30 min, whip whites – Add ½ C sugar until stiff (soft peaks), Set Aside."
  - "4 yolks – whip 1C sugar, lighter color & thick Add ½ tsp salt and 2 tsp B. pwdr"
  - "while mixer is going around Add hot Boiling water – ½C, Add 1 tsp vanilla 1¾ Flour – mix well."
  - "Pour dough over whites & fold in – \"No streaks of yellow\""
  - "No greasing of pan."
  - "Pour into a 9X13 pan"
  - "(cake is done when cake springs back when touched)"
  - "Dip cake squares in frosting & Roll in ground peanuts"

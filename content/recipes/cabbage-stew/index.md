---
title: "Cabbage Stew"
summary: "Ground beef browned with onions, celery and shredded cabbage, then simmered with tomatoes, kidney beans, vinegar and seasonings."
people: ["Grandma Vi"]
categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["cabbage", "ground beef", "kidney beans", "stew"]
yield: "Serves 12 people"
time: "45 min"
time_label: "Simmer"

ingredients:
  - line: "1 lb gr beef"
  - line: "3 onions diced"
  - line: "4 stalks celery"
  - line: "½ head cabbage shredded"
  - line: "1 qt tomatoes"
  - line: "3 cups kidney beans"
  - line: "1 c water"
  - line: "1 T sugar"
  - line: "1 tsp salt"
  - line: "½ tsp pepper"
  - line: "garlic or garlic powder"
  - line: "Soy sauce – dash"
  - line: "2-3 T vinegar"

steps:
  - "Brown 1 lb gr beef"
  - "Add 3 onions diced, 4 stalks celery, ½ head cabbage shredded"
  - "Cook & stir til cabbage is light brown & glossy looking"
  - "then add 1 qt tomatoes, 3 cups kidney beans, 1 c water, 1 T sugar, 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper, garlic or garlic powder, Soy sauce – dash, 2-3 T vinegar"
  - "Heat to boiling"
  - "reduce heat"
  - "simmer 45 min"

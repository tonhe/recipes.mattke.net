---
title: "Aunt B's Pita Bread"
summary: "Aunt B's pita bread: white flour and wheat germ, kneaded, cut into 16 pieces, rolled into rounds and baked."
people: ["Aunt B"]
categories: ["Breads & rolls"]
tags: ["bread", "rolls", "yeast"]
oven: "350°"
time: "About 20 min"

ingredient_groups:
  - ingredients:
      - line: "4 to 5 cups enriched white flour"
      - line: "1 C Wheat Germ"
      - line: "Two pkg's dry yeast"
  - ingredients:
      - line: "2 cup's water"
      - line: "¼ C oil"
      - line: "1 Tblspn Sugar"
      - line: "2 tsp salt"

steps:
  - "Stir together 2 C's Flour & yeast;"
  - "Heat water, oil, Sugar & salt in microwave (about 2 min)"
  - "Add liquid ingredients to flour-yeast mixture & Beat until Smooth. (3 min)"
  - "Stir in more flour (little at a time) to make a moderately soft dough."
  - "Turn out onto lightly floured surface & Knead until smooth & satiny, About 5-10 min."
  - "Cover dough with bowl & let rest 30 min."
  - "Shape into a nice round pat, cut into 16 equal pieces & roll each into a thin round."
  - "Place on greased Baking sheets. (Not Pam – shortening)"
  - "Let rise in warm place – 20 min – until they start to rise a little bit."
  - "Bake – preheated oven at 350° on Bottom rack About 20 min or until very light brown."
  - "(at half time – 10 min, switch pans around top – bottom,)"

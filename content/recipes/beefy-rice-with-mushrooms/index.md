---
title: "Beefy Rice with Mushrooms"
summary: "Rice baked with browned beef, mushrooms, beef broth, consommé, French onion soup and butter."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["beef", "rice", "mushrooms", "casserole"]
oven: "350°"
time: "45 min, or until tender"

ingredients:
  - line: "2c rice"
  - line: "2 cans mushrooms"
  - line: "2c beef broth (low sodium)"
  - line: "1 can beef consommé"
  - line: "1 can French onion soup"
  - line: "4 Tbsp butter"
  - line: "2 lbs beef, browned"

steps:
  - "Brown the beef."
  - "Put everything in a baking dish and stir it together."
  - "Bake at 350° for 45 minutes, or until the rice is tender."
---

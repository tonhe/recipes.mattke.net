  - line: "Dk meat of chicken only"

  - "Put leg quarters skin side up"
  - "blacken turn over blacken that takes 10-15 min"
  - "Put in baking dish"
  - "Put just a little Sweet Baby Ray's BBQ Sauce on each piece – cover tightly"
  - "Bake at 250° – 2-2½ hrs"
  - "Don't peek"

---
title: "Pork Belly Burnt Ends"
summary: "Cubed pork belly smoked at 250°, braised covered in butter and apple juice until probe tender, then glazed with BBQ sauce on the smoker."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["pork", "pork belly", "smoking", "bbq"]
time: "5 to 6 hours at 250°"
time_label: "Smoke"

ingredients:
  - line: "3 to 4 lb skinless pork belly"
  - line: "Yellow mustard or a little neutral oil, for the binder"
  - line: "Your favorite pork or BBQ rub"
  - line: "2 tablespoons butter, cut into pieces"
  - line: "¼ cup apple juice"
  - line: "½ cup BBQ sauce, plus more if needed"
  - line: "2 tablespoons brown sugar or honey"
    note: "optional"

steps:
  - "**Trim and cube.** Take off any skin and trim away thick, hard pieces of fat. Cut the belly into even 1½-inch cubes so they all finish at the same time."
  - "**Season.** Coat the cubes lightly with mustard or oil and season every side with the rub. Let them sit while the smoker heats to 250°."
  - "**Smoke.** Set the cubes on a wire rack with space between them. Smoke until they're browned, the fat is starting to render, and they read about 165–175° inside, usually 3 to 4 hours. Go by color and temperature, not the clock."
  - "**Braise.** Move the cubes to a foil pan, add the butter and apple juice, and cover tightly with foil. Back on the smoker until a probe slides in with almost no resistance. Start checking around 195°; they often need 200–205°. If the fat still feels chewy, cover them again and keep going. Tenderness is the test, not the number."
  - "**Glaze.** Pour off the rendered fat (save it for cooking). Toss the cubes with the BBQ sauce, and the brown sugar or honey if you like. Return them uncovered for 20 to 30 minutes, until the glaze is tacky and set."
  - "**Rest.** Let them sit 10 to 15 minutes before serving."

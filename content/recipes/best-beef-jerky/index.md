---
title: "Best Beef Jerky"
summary: "Thin slices of flank steak soaked, pressed dry, marinated in soy, Maggi and Worcestershire with liquid smoke and cayenne, then dried in a dehydrator."
people: ["Grandma Vi"]
categories: ["Snacks & sauces"]
tags: ["beef", "jerky", "dehydrator", "marinade"]
time: "4-5 hrs"
time_label: "Dehydrate"

ingredient_groups:
  - ingredients:
      - line: "3 lbs flank steak or other – partially frozen"
  - name: "Marinade"
    ingredients:
      - line: "½ c kikkoman soy sauce"
      - line: "¼ c maggi sauce"
      - line: "¼ c Worcestershire sauce"
      - line: "3 T brown sugar"
      - line: "1 tsp onion powder"
      - line: "1 tsp garlic powder"
      - line: "1 tsp liquid smoke"
      - line: "¼ tsp cayenne pepper"

steps:
  - "Slice ¼ inch thick with grain"
  - "Soak in salt water for ½ hr"
  - "Rinse with cold water"
  - "lay pieces on board and using paper towels squeeze blood out flattening some."
  - "Place in ziplock baggie with marinade and refrigerate 1 hr."
  - "Dehydrate at 160-170 for 4-5 hrs."

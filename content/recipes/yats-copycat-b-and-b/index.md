---
title: "Yats Copycat B&B"
summary: "Corn cooked down in butter and brown sugar with red bell pepper and black beans, thickened into a gravy and served over rice."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Main dishes"]
tags: ["black beans", "corn", "copycat", "rice"]
time: "about 25 min"
time_label: "Cook"

ingredients:
  - line: "1 stick butter"
  - line: "1 can corn"
  - line: "3-4 tablespoons brown sugar"
  - line: "1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced"
  - line: "1 can black beans, rinsed"
  - line: "Salt and pepper"
  - line: "About 3 tablespoons flour"
  - line: "About ½ cup milk"
  - line: "Cooked rice, for serving"

steps:
  - "Melt the butter in a pan and add the corn with a little salt and pepper and the brown sugar. Let it cook down about 10 minutes."
  - "Add the red pepper and the black beans, plus a couple teaspoons of salt and pepper. Simmer on medium 5 to 10 minutes."
  - "**Thicken:** spoon some of the liquid from the pan into a small bowl and whisk in the flour until smooth, then stir it back in. Mixing it separately keeps it from getting chunky."
  - "Stir in the milk to turn it into a gravy and let it cook down a little."
  - "Serve over rice."

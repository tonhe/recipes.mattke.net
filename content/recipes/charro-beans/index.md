---
title: "Charro Beans"
summary: "Pinto beans slow-cooked all day in beef broth with bacon, chorizo and hot dogs, onion, jalapeños, cumin and Mexican oregano."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Sides"]
tags: ["beans", "bacon", "chorizo", "hot dogs", "slow cooker"]
time: "8 to 10 hours on low, 5 to 6 on high"
time_label: "Slow cook"

ingredient_groups:
  - name: "Beans"
    ingredients:
      - line: "2 lbs dried pinto beans"
      - line: "Water for soaking"
  - name: "Meat"
    ingredients:
      - line: "8 oz bacon, diced"
      - line: "8 oz Mexican chorizo"
      - line: "Hot dogs, sliced"
      - line: "Ground sausage"
        note: "sometimes"
  - name: "Vegetables"
    ingredients:
      - line: "1 large white onion, diced"
      - line: "6 cloves garlic, minced"
      - line: "2 to 3 jalapeños or serranos, diced"
  - name: "Liquid"
    ingredients:
      - line: "10 to 12 cups beef broth"
  - name: "Seasonings"
    ingredients:
      - line: "2 bay leaves"
      - line: "2 tsp ground cumin"
      - line: "2 tsp Mexican oregano"
      - line: "1 tsp black pepper"
      - line: "1 tsp smoked paprika"
        note: "optional"
      - line: "Salt to taste"
      - line: "Juice of ½ to 1 lime"
        note: "optional"

steps:
  - "**The night before:** sort and rinse the beans. Cover with several inches of cold water and refrigerate for 8 to 12 hours."
  - "**In the morning,** drain and rinse the beans."
  - "In a skillet over medium heat, cook the bacon until crisp. Brown the chorizo (and the sausage, if using) in the bacon fat."
  - "Add the onion and jalapeños and cook 4 to 5 minutes, until the onion softens. Stir in the garlic and cook another 30 seconds."
  - "Move everything to the slow cooker. Add the beans, 10 cups of beef broth, the bay leaves, cumin, oregano, black pepper and smoked paprika, if using."
  - "**Slow cook** on low for 8 to 10 hours or high for 5 to 6, until the beans are almost tender."
  - "About 30 to 45 minutes before serving, stir in the hot dogs and keep cooking until they're heated through and the beans are fully tender. If it gets thicker than you'd like, stir in another cup or two of warm broth."
  - "Take out the bay leaves. Taste before adding salt; between the broth, bacon and chorizo, it may not need much."
  - "If it needs a little lift, stir in the juice of half to one lime just before serving."
---

If your slow cooker has a sear/sauté setting, you can brown the meat and vegetables right in it. This makes a lot: two pounds of beans and 10 cups of broth grow as they cook, so use an 8-quart slow cooker, or cut the recipe in half for a 6-quart.

---
title: "Charro Beans"
summary: "Pinto beans simmered with bacon, chorizo and sliced hot dogs, onion, jalapeños, tomato and cilantro."
people: ["Tony Mattke"]
categories: ["Sides"]
tags: ["beans", "bacon", "chorizo", "hot dogs"]
time: "2 to 2½ hours"
time_label: "Simmer"

ingredient_groups:
  - name: "Beans and meat"
    ingredients:
      - line: "1 lb dried pinto beans, rinsed and soaked"
      - line: "8 oz bacon, diced"
      - line: "8 oz Mexican chorizo"
      - line: "Hot dogs, sliced"
        alt: "or diced ham"
      - line: "Ground sausage"
        note: "sometimes"
  - name: "Vegetables"
    ingredients:
      - line: "1 white onion, chopped"
      - line: "4-6 garlic cloves, minced"
      - line: "2-3 jalapeños or serrano chiles, diced"
      - line: "2 Roma tomatoes, diced"
      - line: "Fresh cilantro, chopped"
  - name: "Liquid and spices"
    ingredients:
      - line: "6 cups chicken stock or bean broth"
        alt: "or 12 oz beer"
      - line: "Salt, black pepper, cumin and dried oregano, to taste"
      - line: "Bay leaves"

steps:
  - "Soak the beans overnight, then drain and rinse."
  - "In a large pot or Dutch oven, cook the bacon until crisp. Lift it out and leave the fat in the pot."
  - "Brown the chorizo (and the ground sausage, if using) in the bacon fat, breaking it up. Add the hot dogs and let them brown a little."
  - "Add the onion and jalapeños and cook until soft, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic for the last minute, then the tomatoes."
  - "Add the beans, the stock, bay leaves, cumin and oregano. Bring to a boil, then turn down to a simmer."
  - "Simmer partly covered for 2 to 2½ hours, until the beans are tender. Add water if it gets too thick."
  - "Stir the bacon back in. Season with salt and pepper, take out the bay leaves, and stir in the cilantro."
---
